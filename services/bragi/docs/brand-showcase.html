Adjura

Name the destination, not the feature.

Adjura — from adjudicate / adjuster, the act of deciding a claim. The brand points at the north star (agentic settlement), not a single feature. Warm, coined, ownable, and unlike anything in the category. Below: the theme and three logo directions. name & .com / trademark to be verified

1 · Theme — breaking the navy wall

Teal leads · amber is the signature · navy becomes the canvas

Every one of 18 competitors is navy/blue. We promote teal to primary (trust + modernity), make amber the signature warmth almost no one uses, keep navy as a near-black surface, and reserve violet for the AI story. Risk states never rely on red/green alone.
