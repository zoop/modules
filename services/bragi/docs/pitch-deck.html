Why now

Hard clocks

Cashless health SLAs: pre-auth ≤ 1 hour, final ≤ 3 hours. Manual review is untenable at scale.

AI blessed

IRDAI named claims + fraud its #1 AI-governance priority, pushing explainable, auditable AI — our exact posture.

The insight

Fraud lives in the mismatch between the photos and the paperwork.

A staged accident has clean photos. A doctored estimate has clean photos too. The lie shows up across the inputs — a policy that lapsed before the loss, a chassis number that doesn't match Vahan, an estimate total that doesn't add up.

Every vendor reads one side. We read both.

The product

One request in. One report out.

  • In: vehicle photos & video + claim documents, any mix — by capture link, dashboard upload, or API.
  • Out: a damage report, a fraud score, and the red flags behind it — each with a reason.
  • Delivered as a PDF and as JSON, into your dashboard and your systems.
  • The engine never approves or rejects. It tells your team where to look.
